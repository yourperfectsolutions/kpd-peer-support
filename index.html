About the KPD Peer Support Group

Our Mission

The KPD Peer Support Program provides confidential, voluntary assistance to law enforcement personnel and their families. Our mission is to reduce stigma around mental health support in police culture while providing immediate, accessible resources for officers in need.

Confidentiality Framework

Peer support conversations remain confidential except in specific circumstances involving imminent danger, child abuse, or court-ordered disclosure. We maintain transparent communication about privacy boundaries to build trust and set clear expectations.

Professional Training

All peer support specialists complete comprehensive training including Critical Incident Stress Management (CISM), Mental Health First Aid, and specialized certifications relevant to their areas of expertise.
