About Our Program

The Killeen Police Department Peer Support Program provides confidential, professional assistance to law enforcement personnel and their families. Our trained peer supporters understand the unique challenges faced by those who serve and protect our community.

Confidential

All conversations are strictly confidential and protected

Professional

Trained and certified peer support specialists

Important Notice

Confidentiality: All peer support conversations are confidential and protected under Texas law. Information shared with peer supporters is not discoverable in legal proceedings.

Not Professional Therapy: Peer support services are not a substitute for professional mental health treatment. We can provide referrals to licensed mental health professionals when appropriate.

Immediate Help

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact emergency services immediately.
